Sheet setup (mm)
Printing checklist
In the print dialog set:
• Paper size → A4
• Margins → None
• Scale → 100% (never “Fit to page”)
• Headers and footers → off
• Background graphics → on (only needed for outlines / emoji)

Do one test on plain paper, hold it against the sticker sheet up to the light, then nudge the margins by a few tenths of a mm if your printer is off.
