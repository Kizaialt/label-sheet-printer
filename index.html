Colour tags
Hover a colour to spotlight it on the sheet. Click one with labels selected to tag them. Tags show as a dot on screen and never print unless you turn that on below.
Logo
Type
Shrink reduces the type until the text fits the label. Changes apply to the selection immediately.
Sheet
Printing & shortcuts
In the print dialog: A4 paper, margins None, scale 100% (never “fit to page”), headers and footers off. Turn on background graphics only if you are printing outlines or dots.

Run one test on plain paper, hold it against the sticker sheet up to the light, then nudge the margin fields by a few tenths of a mm if your printer sits off-centre.
